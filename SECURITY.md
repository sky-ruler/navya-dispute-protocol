## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security and integrity of the Navya Protocol seriously, including the cryptographic lot passports and sensor telemetry audit records.

If you discover a security vulnerability:
1. **Do not create a public issue**.
2. Email details to: `skyruler3281@gmail.com`
3. Include:
   - Nature of the vulnerability
   - Steps to reproduce
   - Potential impact on cryptographic crate verification or dispute settlements
4. We will acknowledge receipt within 48 hours and work with you to triage and release a patch.
